Road Damage Reference Library

Standard surface anomaly categories recognized by the CNN model.
● Severe Hazard

Pothole Depression

Bowl-shaped depressions or holes in the pavement resulting from moisture penetration and traffic stress. High risk of tire blowout, wheel rim warping, and loss of vehicle control.

● Structural Wear

Surface & Longitudinal Cracks

Interconnected fractures and thermal fissures across asphalt pavement. Precursor to severe potholes if left unsealed during freeze-thaw weather cycles.

System Pipeline Architecture

How RoadVision AI localizes and classifies roadway anomalies.

01. Tile Generation

The image or sampled video frame is decomposed into multi-scale overlapping square tiles. Each tile covers candidate pavement sections to pinpoint localized defects.

02. Deep CNN Inference

Extracted tiles are resized to 224×224 and fed into our trained Convolutional Neural Network backbone. Predictions yield class probability vectors across crack, normal, and pothole.

03. Connected Clustering

Adjacent positive tiles are aggregated through 8-way connected-component masking. Outliers are filtered, generating tight bounding boxes with cyber corner accents.

Advisory Note: This neural network prototype is designed for automated decision support and preliminary highway scanning. It does not replace structural civil engineering certification. Always conduct manual inspection for mission-critical pavement remediation.
